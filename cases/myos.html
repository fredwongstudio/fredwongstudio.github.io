MYOS

MYOS — My Operating System: a personal innovation OS

What it is

My own personal innovation OS — a small web app for capturing ideas and reviewing them later. If the studio’s public work is about other people’s problems, MYOS is the tooling for my own stream of them.

The problem

Idea capture usually dies between the moment of thought and the moment of review. I wanted one place that takes an idea as fast as I can say it, and lets me find it again later.

Approach

Three moves, each built in a single pass: capture entries by browser voice capture, keep everything in browser storage (no account, no server), and review them as a personal timeline with filters over the entries.

Tech

  • A self-contained HTML/CSS/JS application — two single-file versions, no dependencies and no back end
  • Browser voice capture for entry; localStorage for persistence
