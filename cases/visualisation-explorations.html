3D VISUALISATION EXPLORATIONS

Spatial design technology exploration

What I did

I explored new 3D visualisation tools by creating highly photorealistic interior environments — a two-room flexi flat and a four-room BTO — and published each rendered gallery as its own small page.

What it taught me

Working hands-on with the design process revealed the friction surrounding the creative work — from measurements and take-offs to documentation, quotations and proposal preparation. That friction later informed several of my workflow experiments, including the calculator and quotation tools in Digital Workflow Tools.
