HUMAN SIGNAL

Enterprise intelligence prototype

The computer knows what was recorded. The humans know what actually happened.

What it is

A prototype exploring a simple idea: what if the operational knowledge, frustrations, observations and suggestions of employees could become structured enterprise data?

The problem

Enterprise systems capture transactions, workflows, finance, operations, supply chain, CRM and sensors. But much of what is actually happening on the ground never becomes data. Repetitive manual work, workarounds, missing information, recurring friction, customer pain — people complain to colleagues, work around the problem, and carry on. The organisation never sees the signal.

Approach

Employees get two simple ways to contribute: something isn’t working (report friction, pain, inefficiency or a workaround) and I have an idea (suggest an improvement). People speak or type naturally; AI does the heavy lifting — capture, structure, connect, synthesise. One complaint is an anecdote; thousands of structured observations can become a new enterprise data layer.

The prototype demonstrates one operational loop: capture the pain, structure it into operational evidence, reveal recurring patterns, synthesise human evidence with system data, let a better operational question emerge — then measure whether the intervention actually made the work better. A standing rule: human input is evidence, not automatically truth. The original source is preserved and tested against enterprise system data.

Why I built it

I built Human Signal as a proof of work for a Palantir Deployment Strategist application. It reflects how I approach problems: what are we trying to solve? Is this even a problem? Is this actually the problem? The job is not to start with the technology or the dashboard — it is to get close to reality, connect fragmented evidence, find the question that matters, and turn that understanding into action.
